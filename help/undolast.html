Undo Last

Undo Last prepares a reverse session from the last GO. Disk files are not changed until you press GO again.

  • Choose MFR → Undo last or press Ctrl+Z when a last operation is available.
  • The Rename List is replaced with reverse OldValues from the log, and the Filter Chain is cleared. Review the preview, then GO to write the undo.
  • Names, paths (moves), attributes, dates, and audio tags recorded in the operation can be reversed this way. Items must still be at the logged locations.
  • For older operations, open the Rename Log and use Undo there (same prepare path).
  • Last-operation undo remains available even when Rename Log retention is disabled in Options.
