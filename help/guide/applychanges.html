Apply Changes (GO)

When the Rename List and Filter Chain look right in preview, apply the changes permanently.

  • Press Ctrl+G, click GO on the toolbar, or choose MFR → GO.
  • Valid renames are committed. Unchanged and preview-error rows are skipped as shown in the preview. You may be warned before GO continues past preview errors.
  • A progress dialog appears for longer operations; you can cancel while it is running.
  • Manual field overrides on rows that were applied (or hit a commit error) are cleared; preview-error and skipped rows keep theirs.
  • Reverse the last GO with Undo Last, or use the Rename Log for earlier operations.
