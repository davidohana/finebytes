How to get a GeoNames username

Nearby place tokens (<geo-*>) call the free GeoNames web service. Options → Location → GeoNames username defaults to the built-in Magic File Renamer account (fbmfr) so lookups work without setup. That account shares a daily credit pool across all installs. Change the field to your own username if you want a private quota.

  1. Create a free account at geonames.org.
  2. Open Manage account / your account settings and enable free web services (required; registration alone is not enough).
  3. Copy your GeoNames username (not your password) into Options → Location → GeoNames username (replacing the default fbmfr).

Credits and attribution

  • Free accounts have hourly and daily credit limits. Each findNearby call costs several credits. Cached coordinates do not spend credits again.
  • The built-in account is shared: heavy use of Nearby on GPS-diverse lists can exhaust the free tier for everyone until the window resets. Prefer your own username for large batches.
  • GeoNames data is Creative Commons Attribution (CC-BY). Credit GeoNames when you publish derived place names.
