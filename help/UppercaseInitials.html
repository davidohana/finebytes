Uppercase Initials

Uppercases initials-style patterns: single letters separated by dots (for example u.s.a or d.j). Other text is left as-is.

Options

This filter has no options.

Examples

bruce springsteen - born in the u.s.a >>> bruce springsteen - born in the U.S.A live in the u.k and the u.s.a and e.u >>> live in the U.K and the U.S.A and E.U initials a.b and c.d.e >>> initials A.B and C.D.E
