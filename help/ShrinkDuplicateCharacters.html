Shrink Duplicate Characters

Collapses runs of two or more of a chosen character into a single occurrence (for example --- → -).

Options

  • Replaces duplicate occurrences of the following character with a single occurrence — The character to shrink. An empty box leaves the name unchanged.

Examples

Song---Title >>> Song-Title a___b__c >>> a_b_c
