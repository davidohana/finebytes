Preset Manager

Preset Manager lists saved presets, loads one into the Filter Chain, renames or edits descriptions, and deletes presets. See also Save a preset.

  • Open from the Presets button above the Filter Chain (or Filters menu).
  • Presets are JSON under your AppData finebytes MFR folder (not MFR 7 .mps / XML). Sample presets can be imported when offered.
  • Select a preset to see its description. Load with Load or double-click. Delete removes the selected preset file(s).
  • You can also load quickly from the Presets drop-down on the Filter Chain toolbar without opening the manager.
