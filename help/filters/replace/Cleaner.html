Cleaner

Replaces characters in the selected text with a single replacement string.

  • Can target illegal file-name characters and/or a custom character list.
  • Replacement is often one safe character, or empty to delete.

Options

  • Replace invalid characters — When checked, replaces characters that are illegal in file names.
  • Custom characters — Additional characters to replace.
  • Replacement — Text inserted in place of each matched character (may be empty).

Examples

Replace invalid characters; replacement _

Massive Attack/Mezzanine >>> Massive Attack_Mezzanine

Custom characters @#; replacement -

DJ@Club#Live >>> DJ-Club-Live
