Index > Filters > Space > Remove Spaces

Remove Spaces

Removes every occurrence of the current word separator from the selected text. The separator comes from Space Character (default: ordinary space).

Options

This filter has no options.

Examples

Word separatorspace (default)

Me And Mom >>> MeAndMom

Word separator_ (via Space Character)

Gone_With_The_Wind >>> GoneWithTheWind
