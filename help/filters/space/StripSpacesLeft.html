Strip Spaces Left

Removes the word separator from the start of the selected text only. The separator comes from Space Character (default: space).

Options

This filter has no options.

Examples

Word separator: space (default)

New_York__.jpg >>> New_York__.jpg

Word separator: _ (via Space Character)

__New_York__ >>> New_York__
