Time Shifter

Shifts a filesystem timestamp by an integer amount.

  • Target may be creation, last write, or last access.
  • Negative amounts move earlier; positive amounts move later.

Options

  • Timestamp field — Creation, last write, or last access.
  • Amount — Integer shift (negative = earlier).
  • Unit — Seconds, minutes, hours, days, months, or years.
