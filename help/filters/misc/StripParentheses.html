Strip Parentheses

Removes one kind of bracket pair: round (), square [], curly {}, or angle &lt;&gt;. You can delete only the delimiters or the whole bracketed region.

Matched pairs are stripped innermost-first; unmatched brackets are left alone.

Options

  • Bracket type — Round, square, curly, or angle brackets.
  • Remove contents — When checked, delete delimiters and text inside; otherwise delete only the bracket characters.

Examples

Round parentheses; remove contents

Song (Live) >>> Song

Round parentheses; remove delimiters only

Song (Live) >>> Song Live

Square brackets; remove contents

Track [Remastered] >>> Track
