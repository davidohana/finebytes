Regular Expressions

Regular expressions are used in the Replacer and Replace List filters to match a pattern of text instead of a fixed literal.

For the full pattern language, see Microsoft’s regular expression language reference.

Text that matched the pattern can be reused in the Replace With field: $0 is the whole match; $1…$9 are captured groups created with (…).

In the filter options, the insert helpers next to the search and replace boxes insert common regex fragments into the focused field.
