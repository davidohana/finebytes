Space Around

Inserts the word separator before and after each trigger character when it is missing on that side.

Options

  • Characters — Characters that should be surrounded by the word separator (for example -).
  • Only when neighboring are letters or digits — Evaluates each side independently.

Examples

Aimee Mann-Stupid Thing >>> Aimee Mann - Stupid Thing Aimee Mann- Stupid Thing >>> Aimee Mann - Stupid Thing
