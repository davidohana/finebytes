Filters > Replace > Cleaner

Cleaner

Replaces invalid file-name characters and/or custom characters you list with a single replacement string (often one safe character, or empty to delete).

Options

  • Replace invalid characters — When checked, replaces characters that are illegal in file names.
  • Custom characters — Additional characters to replace.
  • Replacement — Text inserted in place of each matched character (may be empty).

Examples

a/b >>> a_b a@b#c >>> a-b-c
